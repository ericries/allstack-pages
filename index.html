About the paper

Built from the work
of independent writers.

We are developing a newspaper that summarizes, analyzes and links to published work. The aim is to give readers a useful daily account of their world, and give the writers behind it a wider audience.

The planned paper will use automated tools and will do no original reporting. Stories will identify their sources and link readers to the original work. More extensive excerpts and full creator articles will require permission.
